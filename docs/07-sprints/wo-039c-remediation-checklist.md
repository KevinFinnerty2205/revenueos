# WO-039C real-data operations remediation checklist

Status: authoritative pre-change checklist
Branch: `feature/pre-beta-wo-039c-real-data-operations`
Baseline: `5f0a61c` (`main` and `origin/main` at audit start)
Prepared: 31 August 2026 (Australia/Sydney)
Scope boundary: WO-039C only. WO-040 is not authorised.

## Purpose and completion rule

This checklist was written before production-code changes for WO-039C. It is the release-control record for moving RevenueOS from a synthetic-only beta posture to a supervised, restricted real-data design-partner posture.

Every row must finish in exactly one of these states before the work order is handed off:

- `FIXED` — the implementation, automated proof and operator evidence named in the row exist and agree.
- `NOT REPRODUCED` — the stated risk was disproved with durable evidence recorded in this document.
- `DEFERRED WITH JUSTIFICATION` — the item is outside WO-039C, has an explicit owner and containment, and does not block the claimed readiness level.

A real-data safety, tenant-isolation, recovery, deletion, provisioning or provider-governance blocker may not be deferred while claiming readiness for real customer data. Passing unit tests alone is not sufficient evidence for an environment or operator claim.

## Mandatory implementation order

Work proceeds in this order and may not skip forward around an unresolved blocker:

1. Production safety, identity and tenant-isolation controls (C01–C05).
2. Backup/restore, retention/export/deletion, provisioning/offboarding and operations (C06–C12).
3. Dependency, feature/provider and legal gates (C13–C16).
4. Native CRM import and merge (C17–C18).
5. Synthetic onboarding drill and readiness decision (C19–C20).

## Initial release posture

The pre-change posture is **NO-GO for real customer data**. This is not a forecast of the final WO-039C decision. It records that the confirmed open blockers above have not yet been remediated or proven in a named target environment.
