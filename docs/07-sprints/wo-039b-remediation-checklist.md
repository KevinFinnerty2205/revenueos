preview → deterministic generation → structural validation → human approval → secure
PowerPoint download chain. WO-039C operations/import, WO-040+, Gmail, Apollo, other
providers, DOCX, Google Slides, arbitrary Office rendering and paid scanning remain
outside this work order.
