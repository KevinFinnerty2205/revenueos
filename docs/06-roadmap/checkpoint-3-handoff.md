# Checkpoint 3 — Product/platform validation handoff

- **Status:** Ready for evaluation after WO-039; no go/no-go decision recorded
- **Date:** 30 August 2026

## Decision required

Evaluate Core, Prospect, Engage, Create and native CRM/Pipeline both as distinct
commercial propositions and as one connected RevenueOS system. For each, decide
**keep, modify, defer or remove** before authorising WO-040+ ecosystem work or making
broader beta claims.

## Evidence to collect

- activation and repeat Core use across the first design-partner cohort;
- whether Daily, Pipeline and Manager Intelligence reduce CRM/forecast preparation;
- seller trust in transparent categories and manager-view visibility;
- manager trust in deal-attention reasons, source lineage and discussion questions;
- forecast category realisation and historical-baseline sparse-data behaviour;
- Target/Actual/Forecast comprehension without blended or causal claims;
- Prospect research usefulness and correction burden;
- Engage review/safety value before external send capability expands;
- Create output quality and approved-template/value-model operating cost;
- native CRM replacement versus complement evidence;
- tenant permission, privacy, export/erasure, support and operational burden; and
- willingness to pay for each proposition and the connected system.

## Manager Intelligence validation

Test with administrators and affected sellers. A manager should identify a material
deal conversation faster than inspecting CRM notes, explain every surfaced condition,
understand seller/manager/RevenueOS perspectives as separate and know the next click.
The seller should see manager judgment on their own deal without hidden evaluation.
Remove signals that are not fair, source-backed and useful.

Reject any direction that introduces people scoring, ranking, comparative personal
Target attainment, behavioural surveillance, persistent employee coaching profiles,
opaque AI coaching or a hidden blended forecast. Admin-as-manager is an explicit v1
limitation; validate the need and safe shape of hierarchy before designing one.

## Exit gate

Proceed only with a recorded decision covering proposition scope, target cohort,
chosen first productivity/CRM ecosystem, privacy/operational gates, success measures
and work to remove or simplify. An inconclusive checkpoint pauses or narrows Stage D;
it does not silently authorise connectors, Deal Rooms, handover or beta readiness.
