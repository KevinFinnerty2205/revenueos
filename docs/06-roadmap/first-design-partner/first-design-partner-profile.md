# Ideal first RevenueOS design partner

- **Purpose:** select a company later; no company is selected or approved here
- **Product test:** supervised Core RevenueOS/Sales Brain value, Native CRM first
- **Status:** owner-approved profile required before outreach/agreement; no real data may be requested during selection

## Concise target profile

The ideal first partner is an Australian B2B, relationship-led sales team with **3–15
salespeople** and an accessible founder/CEO or sales leader. It has recurring,
meaningful sales conversations; currently relies on spreadsheets, a basic CRM or a
CRM workflow the team finds administratively heavy; and wants better meeting-to-action
follow-through, opportunity visibility and a consistent sales rhythm.

For the first 6–8 weeks it can nominate 3–5 active users, one accountable admin and a
senior sponsor. It is willing to start with RevenueOS Native CRM, deliberately paste
or select authorised plain-text transcripts, review AI output rather than trust it
automatically, attend a weekly feedback session and report friction candidly.

It does **not** need Gmail, Apollo, HubSpot, Salesforce, recording, live meeting
capture, SSO/SCIM, mobile apps, autonomous actions or a contractual SLA to discover
value.

## Must-have selection criteria

| Criterion | Evidence to obtain without receiving customer data |
| --- | --- |
| Team and motion | 3–15 salespeople; consultative B2B motion with regular calls/meetings and opportunities that evolve over days/weeks rather than one-click transactions |
| Sponsor access | Founder/CEO/sales leader can make decisions, join onboarding and attend a short weekly review |
| Working cohort | 3–5 users and one accountable admin can use the product during agreed supervised hours |
| Core pain | Clear pain in meeting follow-up, CRM admin, opportunity visibility, pipeline discipline or turning evidence into next action |
| Native CRM fit | Willing to begin with a small reviewed CSV import or manual subset and use RevenueOS as the beta working record; no connector dependency |
| Data authority | Can attest it has authority to provide the selected ordinary business CRM data and deliberately supplied transcript content; will start with the minimum subset |
| Feedback behaviour | Agrees to Day 1/Week 1/Week 2/Month 1 checkpoints, weekly candid feedback and immediate reporting of confusing or contradictory state |
| Risk fit | Ordinary business sales data only; no highly regulated, special-category or unusually sensitive use case; can accept disclosed subprocessors/cross-border AI if enabled |
| Operational fit | Accepts invitation-only access, supervised support, current browser boundary, no SLA/HA claim and tested offboarding/export/deletion process |

## Strong positive signals

- the team has tried to build sales rhythm with spreadsheets/templates but loses
  context or follow-up;
- leaders still coach deals by asking reps for manual updates;
- sales conversations contain decisions, actions, risks, objections and stakeholder
  evidence worth reviewing;
- users are motivated to improve the workflow, not merely obtain free software;
- the partner can supply synthetic examples first and is comfortable limiting the
  initial real import to 5 Accounts, about 10 Contacts and about 5 open Opportunities;
- there is no procurement committee or integration programme required to start.

## Avoid for the first partner

- bank, insurer, hospital/health provider, government agency, school/child-focused
  service or another heavily regulated/special-category data environment;
- legal, financial-advice or other professional workflow likely to place privileged
  or highly sensitive client content into transcripts without mature controls;
- very large enterprise, complex Salesforce/Dynamics estate, multi-subsidiary tenant
  model or organisation requiring data migration at scale;
- any organisation requiring SSO/SCIM, strict Australian-only processing, 24/7
  support, contractual availability/response SLA, penetration-test pack or High
  Availability at launch;
- a team whose value depends on Gmail/Outlook send, Apollo/lead data, HubSpot sync,
  recording/transcription, live intelligence, mobile apps or autonomous execution;
- transactional/consumer sales with little relationship or meeting evidence;
- a sponsor who delegates all contact, cannot attend weekly reviews or wants an
  unsupervised rollout;
- a prospect seeking a production CRM replacement commitment, custom feature build
  or final pricing promise before the design partnership produces evidence.

## Selection question

Select the company that best answers **yes** to this: “Can 3–5 people use Native CRM
and the reviewed meeting-to-action Sales Brain loop every week for 6–8 weeks, with a
decision-maker present, using ordinary authorised B2B sales data and no external
integration dependency?”

If OpenAI is not approved, the candidate must also accept that the initial
`NATIVE-NO-EXTERNAL-AI-V1` phase tests Core workflow only and is not the full Sales
Brain value test.

## Partner selection does not authorise data

Discovery may collect ordinary company-fit information and agreement feedback. Do
not request, receive, preview or enter CRM exports, transcripts, contact lists,
customer names or other partner data until the named target has passed every owner,
legal, technical and partner gate.
