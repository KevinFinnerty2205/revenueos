# Getting started with RevenueOS

RevenueOS is your sales workflow and relationship-intelligence workspace. For this supervised design-partner pilot, it uses Native CRM and only the capabilities agreed with your administrator. Prospect research, live email sending, Campaign auto-send and Event outreach are not enabled.

## Your first session

1. **Home:** start with the important work and upcoming Interactions shown for you.
2. **Accounts:** find the business relationship and its recent context.
3. **Contacts:** check the people attached to the Account. A stored work email does not mean the person can be contacted.
4. **Interactions:** deliberately capture or add authorised customer-conversation information. RevenueOS never records implicitly.
5. **Sales Brain:** review what RevenueOS found. Check its source and uncertainty; correct or reject anything that is wrong.
6. **Opportunity:** keep the deal, Evidence, Methodology gaps and Actions together.
7. **Pipeline:** review current open Opportunities. Imported deals begin at their current stage; RevenueOS does not invent earlier stage history.

Once that loop is comfortable, your second session covers **Insights**, **Targets** and **Forecast**. Seller and manager forecasts remain separate judgments; RevenueOS does not create a hidden score or rank people.

**Create** is optional. Use it only if your agreed pilot includes approved templates and private file handling. Prospect and Engage live-provider features are not part of this pilot.

## Import and trust

Your administrator will begin with about 5 Accounts, 10 Contacts and 5 open Opportunities. Check names, owners, links, stages, amounts, currencies, duplicates and contact restrictions before approving a larger import. RevenueOS will not automatically merge uncertain duplicates or treat imported data as customer-confirmed Evidence.

Only upload ordinary business sales data needed for the pilot. Never upload passwords, payment-card data, government IDs, health information, private personal dossiers or unrelated HR information. See the short [data boundary](partner-data-boundary.md).

## Help and feedback

Use the approved support address and include the page, time and visible request ID. Do not email customer data, screenshots of sensitive content, CSV rows, transcripts, documents or credentials unless support explicitly authorises a secure route.

We will ask what was confusing, what saved time, what you ignored, what you still did elsewhere and what you did not trust. The pilot is about reducing work and improving the sales workflow—not monitoring individual activity.
