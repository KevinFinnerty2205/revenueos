# Design-partner data boundary

## Do upload

Only the minimum business information needed for the approved RevenueOS workflow:

- business Accounts;
- business Contacts and work contact details;
- open sales Opportunities;
- customer sales Interactions you are authorised to process;
- commercial documents needed for an explicitly enabled workflow; and
- synthetic examples during setup and drills.

Use current, authorised source exports. Review every mapped field. An imported work email does **not** give permission to contact that person.

## Do not upload

- passwords, API keys, access tokens or other credentials;
- payment-card or bank-account data;
- government identifiers or identity-document images;
- health, medical or other sensitive-category information;
- unnecessary sensitive personal information;
- private personal dossiers, private social data or personality profiles;
- unrelated HR, performance, disciplinary or recruitment information; or
- data you do not have authority to share and process.

Do not upload a whole data warehouse, inbox, drive or historical CRM export “just in case”. Remove unsupported columns and unrelated records first. Ask the named support contact before upload when classification or authority is uncertain.
