# First design-partner commercial model

- **Recommendation:** **FREE DESIGN PARTNER**
- **Term:** 6–8 weeks, fixed and reviewable
- **Status:** owner/legal approval and signed partner agreement required; no billing implementation

The first partner should not be used to set final RevenueOS pricing. The product is
ready only for a supervised real-data design partnership after target proof—not an
unsupervised or commercial beta. Integrations remain limited, support is manual and
the partner accepts early-product risk. Charging now would create expectations that
RevenueOS cannot yet support reliably.

## Options evaluated

| Model | Benefit | Problem at this stage | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free design partnership | Maximises candid workflow feedback, reduces procurement friction and matches the supervised/limited product | RevenueOS bears target/provider/support cost; free users still need real commitment | **Recommended with a written time/feedback commitment** |
| Discounted beta | Tests willingness to pay and creates some commitment | Suggests commercial readiness/pricing before reliability, support and value are evidenced | Not recommended for the first partner; reconsider after observed use |
| Paid pilot | Strongest economic signal | Creates procurement, SLA, security, deliverable and refund expectations disproportionate to the current release status | Do not use for the first partner |

## Recommended offer boundary

Subject to owner-approved legal documents, offer one company:

- no RevenueOS licence fee for a fixed 6–8-week design partnership;
- 3–5 active users within one invitation-only organisation;
- supervised Native CRM onboarding, small-subset import and agreed support hours;
- `NATIVE-AI-REVIEW-V1` only if OpenAI/partner/target gates pass, otherwise the honest
  no-external-AI profile;
- a weekly 30–45 minute feedback session plus Day 1, Week 1, Week 2 and end-of-term
  review;
- an immediate stop/pause right for either party, with authorised export and verified
  offboarding/deletion;
- no custom integration, data migration at scale, 24/7 support, availability SLA,
  autonomous action, production-volume or roadmap delivery promise; and
- no automatic conversion, renewal, price, discount entitlement or exclusivity after
  the term.

RevenueOS pays approved hosting/Clerk/OpenAI costs within the owner caps. The partner
remains responsible for choosing authorised users, lawful/source-authorised data,
reviewing AI output and not supplying prohibited sensitive content. Free access does
not remove the need for the beta agreement, Privacy Notice, Terms/DPA, subprocessor
disclosure, security boundary and deletion/export commitments.

## Commitment without a fee

The exchange is access and supervised support for evidence and feedback—not customer
endorsement. Before onboarding, the partner should agree to:

- name the executive sponsor, admin and 3–5 users;
- attend onboarding and weekly reviews;
- begin with the bounded subset and review import/AI results;
- permit RevenueOS to collect content-minimised product feedback and operational
  metrics under the approved notice;
- report defects/confusing state promptly and accept pause criteria; and
- decide at the end whether value is strong enough to continue, narrow or stop.

Do not require a testimonial, logo use, case study or reference as consideration.
Each needs separate written approval after value is observed.

## End-of-term decision

At 6–8 weeks, use the existing post-partner roadmap decision: continue/narrow the
Core loop, prioritise one evidenced provider path, fix usability/trust, or stop and
reconsider. Any paid continuation receives a new owner-approved commercial offer and
legal review. No billing code, Stripe account or invoice is needed for this model.
