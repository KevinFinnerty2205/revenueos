# First design-partner feedback and success plan

Status: **WAITING FOR PARTNER**. The purpose is to learn whether RevenueOS reduces work and improves the sales workflow. It is not employee monitoring.

## Success measures

| Outcome | Evidence method | Positive signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supervised onboarding works | Operator observation and import reconciliation | Partner admin completes mapping/preview/confirm without engineering or database intervention |
| Core records are findable | Ask users to find a named sample Account, Contact and Opportunity | They succeed and explain where they would look next |
| Interaction capture is usable | Observe one authorised Interaction workflow | User captures the necessary information without unsafe workarounds |
| Sales Brain is understandable | Ask the user to distinguish source, reviewed Evidence, inference and unknown | They can explain what to trust/correct and identify unsupported information |
| Methodology supports the deal | Review one real workflow with the user | Gaps are useful and do not become invented completion |
| Actions reduce administration | Compare prior process with one reviewed Action | User can create/review/use it and identifies work avoided |
| Pipeline is credible | Reconcile current stage/amount/owner and review one move | User trusts present state and understands import-baseline limits |
| Forecast is understandable | Seller and manager explain Actual, Target, their separate judgments and baseline | No one interprets a hidden probability/score or manager judgment as override |
| Manager deal review improves | Observe preparation for one deal review | Manager finds evidence, gaps and questions faster without judging rep behaviour |
| Product earns a return visit | Ask why the user returned to Home/Daily without prompting | They name a useful workflow/outcome, not a notification or novelty |
| Support is operable | Review issues and request-ID resolutions | Problems are diagnosed without customer content in tickets and within agreed expectations |
| Trust survives correction | Follow one wrong/missing item through correction | User sees later context reflect the correction and remains willing to rely on the workflow |

Measure task completion, observed friction, before/after preparation or administration time estimates, support issues and explicit feedback. Use only privacy-safe product events already approved in the repository, such as content-free workflow/audit states and submitted feedback. Do not add clickstream, screen recording, message/call volume tracking, people ranking or hidden instrumentation for this gate.

No individual rep score, comparative leaderboard, productivity rating, presence monitoring, sentiment/personality assessment or surveillance profile is permitted. Aggregation must not make a person identifiable when the cohort is tiny.

## Interview cadence

### Day 1 — setup and first workflow

Focus on import, finding records, language, trust and immediate blockers. Observe tasks first; ask questions after the user has attempted them.

### End of Week 1 — repeated use

Focus on what they returned to, support load, corrections, missing information and work still done elsewhere.

### End of Week 2 — workflow fit

Focus on Interaction → Evidence → Opportunity/Action/Pipeline/Forecast continuity, manager review and whether the product reduced duplicate entry.

### End of Month 1 — continue/narrow/stop

Focus on sustained value, trust, must-have capability, operational burden and the [post-partner decision](post-partner-roadmap-decision.md).

Ask the same neutral core questions at each suitable session:

1. What was confusing?
2. What saved time?
3. What did you ignore?
4. What did you still do in Excel or another CRM?
5. What did you not trust?
6. What information was wrong or missing?
7. Would you be annoyed if RevenueOS disappeared tomorrow? Why or why not?
8. Which one capability made you return?

Then ask for a concrete recent example. Do not suggest a preferred answer, present a roadmap solution before hearing the problem or ask users to rate colleagues.

## Evidence record and review

Keep a concise research log with participant role, date, workflow attempted, outcome, time/friction estimate, trust/correction issue, support issue, direct product request and decision relevance. Paraphrase sensitive context; do not copy customer sales content. Separate observed behaviour, participant statement and team inference.

Review evidence after each session for immediate pause criteria. At Week 2 and Month 1, classify each major finding as product value, usability, trust/data integrity, operations/support or missing external capability. The Month 1 review selects exactly one post-partner path; Gmail is not automatic.
