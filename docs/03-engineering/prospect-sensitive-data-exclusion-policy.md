# Prospect sensitive-data exclusion policy

Prospect Person Intelligence accepts only professional facts relevant to a legitimate company-scoped sales research task.

The service rejects content about protected or highly personal characteristics, including race or ethnicity, religion, political views, trade-union status, health or disability, genetics or biometrics, sexuality, family, children, home address and private finances. It also rejects personality types, psychographic labels, emotional vulnerability, facial analysis and personal/private interests.

Personal email addresses and personal mobiles are unsupported. Business direct dials are disabled in v1. Profile photos are neither fetched, stored nor rendered. Public accessibility alone does not make content appropriate.

Validation runs before persistence for provider candidates, observations, hypotheses and contact points. Unknown is preferred to a guessed identity or contact value. A future AI/provider synthesiser must treat all retrieved text as untrusted data, use the same bounded schema and exclusions, and never follow instructions embedded in source content.

Violations fail the research result safely and do not echo rejected content into logs or public errors. Security tests cover sensitive rapport content and inferred email rejection.
