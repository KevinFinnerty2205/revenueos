# Business card handling

Business-card images may produce `contact_detail` candidates only. The UI explains that RevenueOS does not save a Contact automatically and does not infer a role, buying authority, stakeholder influence or relationship strength.

The user can edit, accept or reject extracted contact text in the visual review. Accepted contact-detail Evidence remains linked to the interaction and source visual but is excluded from Interaction Intelligence and Revenue Brain. Creating or updating a Contact requires a separate explicit user action that WO-014 does not implement.

This boundary prevents a business card from becoming a buying signal and avoids silent writes to the system of record.
