# Oryntela future product opportunities

- **Status:** Bounded discovery portfolio; none is authorised implementation
- **Consolidated:** 4 September 2026
- **Rule:** Customer evidence and dependency readiness determine order

## High-leverage candidates

| Opportunity                   | Problem removed                                                                                 | Existing primitives reused                                                            | Key risk                                       | Validate when                                                  |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Company & Selling Profile     | Repeated/fragmented explanation of what the customer sells                                      | Organisation context, Target Markets, approved content, Engage offering, Value Models | Duplicate truth and prompt injection           | First-partner output lacks relevance across multiple workflows |
| What Changed                  | Sellers reconstruct meaningful deal movement after time away                                    | Evidence, Revenue Brain history, Methodology, Actions, Forecast staleness             | Noise and surveillance-like last-view tracking | Repeated account/deal reorientation is slow                    |
| Meeting-to-meeting continuity | Commitments and gaps from the last Interaction are missed before the next                       | Interaction history, Evidence, Actions, preparation briefs                            | Repetition and stale context                   | Users repeatedly reconstruct the last meeting manually         |
| Follow-through completeness   | Approved Actions, CRM proposals and communication drafts remain unresolved after an Interaction | Action lifecycle, execution receipts, provider state                                  | Becoming a generic task manager                | Sellers lose customer work despite existing Action records     |
| Customer commitment view      | Customer and seller promises are mixed with ordinary tasks                                      | Action Items, Evidence, source links, due context                                     | Inferring a commitment that was not made       | Partners repeatedly lose explicit promises                     |

The five concepts above are additive ideas, not five separate products. Meeting
continuity, follow-through and commitments may prove to be projections of existing
Evidence and Actions rather than new domain models.

## Priority rule

Score only after customer evidence using frequency of pain, time removed, Core value,
retention/revenue potential, primitive reuse, complexity, security/privacy risk,
provider dependence and variable cost. Trust or data-integrity defects outrank all
feature candidates.

Do not create an issue, epic or work order until a candidate passes its entry signal.
