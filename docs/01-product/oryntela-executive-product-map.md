# Oryntela executive product map

- **Status:** One-page owner summary; strategy only
- **As at:** 4 September 2026

## What is Oryntela?

**Oryntela is the AI operating system for sales.** It helps a relationship-driven
sales team remember what matters, understand the next best action and keep evidence,
commitments, pipeline and forecast connected. RevenueOS remains the technical name
inside the current repository.

## What is Core?

Core is the valuable daily product: Home, Accounts, People, Interactions, Evidence,
Sales Brain, Methodology, Actions, Opportunities, Pipeline, Search/Ask, Analytics,
Targets, Forecast and Manager Intelligence. A customer should not need an add-on to
experience the central Evidence → Sales Brain → Methodology → Action →
Pipeline/Forecast loop.

## What can customers eventually add?

- **Prospect:** find and research relevant companies and decision makers.
- **Engage:** prepare and supervise respectful outreach, campaigns and event follow-through.
- **Create:** produce approved branded presentations and defensible business cases.
- **Native CRM:** use Oryntela as an intentionally simple system of record where an external CRM is unnecessary.

These are product modules, not the proposed customer plan names.

## What is built?

The repository implements the Core domains and experiences above, deterministic
analytics/targets/forecasting, manager attention, Create/Business Cases, Native CRM,
Pipeline, and bounded Prospect/Engage/Event foundations. It also contains focused
HubSpot architecture and private-beta operational hardening. Built does not mean
deployed, provider-live, commercially available or proven with real customer data.

## What still needs a live provider or target proof?

Real external research, mailbox delivery/replies, SMS, voice, production AI and
transcription require separately approved providers and operational proof. The
target environment, authentication, tenant isolation, storage, recovery,
observability and safe-error evidence must pass the first-partner gate before real
data. Prospect is mock-backed and Engage delivery is simulated today.

## What are we validating?

Whether Daily is the natural start; Sales Brain saves meaningful time; deliberate
capture, Methodology, Actions and Ask are trusted; Native CRM/Pipeline are sufficient;
Targets/Forecast/Manager Intelligence improve real conversations; Create saves work;
and Oryntela understands enough about what the partner sells. A concise Company
Selling Profile and a compact Home target/forecast signal are hypotheses, not builds.

## How should the complete workflow feel?

A seller starts on Home and sees what matters. They prepare an account meeting with
approved company context, deliberately capture or supply the interaction, review the
Evidence, inspect Sales Brain and Methodology changes, then approve the next Actions.
Authorised updates flow to the Opportunity; canonical state updates pipeline, target
and forecast context; the manager sees an explainable attention item. If needed, the
seller creates a branded presentation or defensible Business Case. The next meeting
starts better prepared and the eventual outcome informs Analytics and longitudinal
deal memory.

If enabled later, Prospect begins with a Target Market and sourced research, then
requires explicit promotion into Sales. Engage begins with reviewed outreach and
stop conditions. Neither description implies that a live provider exists today.

## First ten minutes principle

A new seller should understand Oryntela's value within ten minutes: see the purpose
on Home, open an Account or Opportunity, inspect Sales Brain, prepare an Interaction,
understand Evidence and Actions, and see relevant pipeline/target context. They
should not configure dozens of settings; administration happens separately.

Potential first-value events are a genuinely useful meeting preparation, a captured
interaction that removes duplicate notes, a correct next action, or an evidence-led
deal risk the team would otherwise miss.

## What are we deliberately not building?

Full Salesforce-style CRM parity, a generic workflow/BI/project-management platform,
employee leaderboards or surveillance, private-life buyer dossiers, a generic
autonomous SDR, bulk spam, a general file repository, native mobile solely for parity,
or opaque scores and forecasts.
