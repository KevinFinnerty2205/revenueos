# Oryntela product principles

- **Status:** Authoritative product invariants
- **Consolidated:** 4 September 2026
- **Scope:** Product and work-order decisions; no implementation authority

1. **Sales Brain at the centre.** A capability must feed, improve or safely act on the
   shared understanding of the customer and deal.
2. **Do the thinking and organising so the salesperson can do the selling.** Remove
   clerical work and decision clutter.
3. **Make the useful action obvious.** A new seller should understand what to do next
   without learning the architecture.
4. **One shared truth.** Reuse canonical Account, Person, Opportunity, Interaction,
   Evidence and Action primitives instead of creating parallel intelligence stores.
5. **Evidence before assertion.** Show origin, support, conflict, freshness and
   uncertainty wherever a conclusion matters.
6. **Tell me what matters; show me why; show me everything.** Use progressive
   disclosure rather than a dashboard wall.
7. **Human authority for consequence.** Customer communication, CRM writes, spend
   and other external effects remain explicit, reviewable and auditable.
8. **No silent certainty.** Unknown, inferred, seller-reported and customer-direct
   information keep distinct authority.
9. **No fabricated numbers.** ROI uses approved assumptions and deterministic maths;
   Forecast preserves judgement and history without arbitrary probability theatre.
10. **No employee surveillance.** Help managers coach deals, not rank people by
    activity, clicks, talk time or hidden scores.
11. **Respect the buyer.** Use relevant public professional context; never build
    private-life dossiers or psychological manipulation.
12. **No uncontrolled outreach.** Contactability, suppression, review, idempotency,
    quotas, provider reconciliation and stop conditions are product requirements.
13. **Core must be great.** Add-ons expand the workflow and never ransom essential
    Sales Brain understanding, provenance or control.
14. **Enter information once.** Reuse approved context safely across preparation,
    research, outreach, Create and business cases.
15. **Opinionated before configurable.** Prefer sales-aware defaults over a generic
    workflow builder, arbitrary custom rules or permanent configuration burden.
16. **Mobile must fit the sales day.** Small-screen and field workflows are first
    class; native software is justified only by a validated platform problem.
17. **External authority survives integration.** Preserve source-of-record ownership,
    unknown delivery state, conflict and reconciliation rather than pretending sync is
    certainty.
18. **Variable cost is explicit and bounded.** No provider goes live without a unit
    model, exposure cap, customer value and positive-margin path.
19. **Trust and privacy are product behaviour.** Tenant isolation, minimisation,
    consent, retention, export, deletion and safe support are not premium extras.
20. **More features are not automatically better.** A major addition must remove a
    repeated customer problem without creating more concepts than it retires.

Every future major work order must complete the
[Oryntela Simplicity Gate](../02-design/oryntela-simplicity-principles.md#work-order-simplicity-gate).
Provider-backed work must also pass the
[variable-cost safety gate](../03-engineering/oryntela-variable-cost-safety-gate.md).
