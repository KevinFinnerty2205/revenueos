# AI Companion preparation

## Current product capability

AI Companion currently helps a salesperson prepare before an Interaction. On an
upcoming Interaction, the user can open or create a concise, versioned brief,
review it on desktop or mobile web and optionally mark it reviewed. The brief does
not listen, record or depend on later capture.

The experience answers:

- what the linked account and opportunity currently show;
- what changed in the latest Revenue Brain comparison;
- which objectives and customer-appropriate questions matter for this interaction;
- which validated stakeholders, commitments, objections and risks deserve focus;
- what observable outcome would make the interaction useful; and
- how preparation should change for the Interaction type.

It supports online and face-to-face meetings, presentations, workshops, site
visits, executive lunches, phone calls, conference interactions, trade-show
interactions and manually classified interactions.

## Trust contract

Preparation uses linked record metadata and existing validated structured
intelligence only. Empty context remains visibly empty. A recommendation is not a
customer fact, source completeness is not win probability, silence is not
resolution and seller presentation material is not customer evidence. The UI does
not expose raw source IDs or AI infrastructure terminology.

## Deliberately not implemented

The current AI Companion has no live mode, AI Debrief, Voice Journal, microphone,
camera, recording, transcription, telephony, dialling, meeting bot, live coaching,
document upload, notification or external action. Those remain future work and
require separate authority, consent and product validation.
