# Prospect research versus customer Evidence

**Status:** Current WO-026 boundary

Prospect Research and customer Evidence answer different questions and remain
different domains.

Prospect Research describes public or provider-supplied context about a company
before or around initial pursuit. Customer Evidence records deliberately supplied
or captured customer interactions, documents, emails, recordings and reviewed
findings. Public research is not proof of what a customer said, agreed, objected to
or committed to.

Promotion links a Research Target to a canonical Company; it does not copy
research observations into Evidence. The Company page exposes a separately labelled
Public research card that links back to the brief. The link does not mutate the
Opportunity Workspace, Methodology fields, Revenue Brain snapshots, Ask RevenueOS
retrieval corpus, Contacts or Opportunities.

Deleting Prospect research removes the target, runs, source metadata, observations
and citation links. If the target was promoted, its canonical Company survives and
can only be deleted through the separate Core Company lifecycle. Organisation
export includes the two domains as separate structures so downstream users cannot
mistake public research for customer-direct truth.

Future Engage personalisation may use appropriately licensed verified or
provider-supplied public professional context and visibly cautious inference, but
must never represent an inference as personal fact. WO-026 introduces no outreach.
