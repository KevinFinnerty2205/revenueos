# Buying Committee Hypothesis guide

Buying Committee Hypotheses help a seller decide who else may be worth understanding. They are deliberately weaker than Stakeholder Intelligence and customer-confirmed sales truth.

## Current roles

WO-027 supports executive sponsor, economic-buyer candidate, champion candidate, business buyer, technical evaluator, security, procurement, legal, finance, end-user influencer and other relevant. Discovery first uses a bounded professional-function mapping. Research may then propose one or more roles from cited public responsibilities and company context.

Each hypothesis stores a cautious rationale, trust state, source links, research-run version and review state. New hypotheses start as `system_hypothesis` and `needs_validation`. A seller can mark one relevant or not relevant; that changes only the Prospect hypothesis to `seller_assessed`.

## Non-propagation rule

A reviewed hypothesis does not create or update a canonical stakeholder, Methodology field, Opportunity fact, Evidence row, Revenue Brain insight or Ask RevenueOS source. Confirmation requires later customer/sales evidence through the relevant domain. Coverage gaps such as “Security owner not yet identified” are research prompts, not proof that a role is absent.
