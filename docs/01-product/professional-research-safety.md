# Professional Research Safety guide

Prospect Person Intelligence should feel reasonable if the researched professional saw it. It is a concise business brief, never a personal dossier.

## Allowed

- source-backed current role, employer, responsibilities and professional history;
- relevant authored articles, public professional posts, interviews, conferences and company initiatives obtained through permitted access;
- cautious professional conversation context grounded in those sources;
- business contact points with field-level trust, expiry and permission warnings.

## Excluded

- private or scraped social content;
- religion, ethnicity, health, disability, politics, sexuality, family, home address or other sensitive/private traits;
- personality, behavioural or emotional profiling;
- personal interests unrelated to work, facial recognition or profile-photo processing;
- inferred real identities, guessed contact details, background monitoring, scoring, ranking or bulk harvesting.

Provider text is untrusted input. The service validates bounded categories, public-safe URLs, source linkage, cautious wording and sensitive-content exclusions before persistence. No raw provider payload or mirrored profile page is retained. Public prospect statements remain separately labelled research and never become customer-direct Evidence.
