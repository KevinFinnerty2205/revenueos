# Oryntela conversation product: acceptance contract

The owner-approved product is a simple combination of meeting notes and sales conversation intelligence, inspired by Backdrop and Granola. GTM teams and startup founders should prepare, take notes or capture an authorised conversation, understand what happened, and follow through. CRM administration, prospecting campaigns, pipeline management and forecasting are not required to use this product.

This contract supersedes earlier CRM-led navigation requirements. Existing tenant records and legacy functionality remain intact; preserving infrastructure does not require exposing it in the everyday product.

## Web workflow in this change

- Home, account directory and Search lead to `/conversations/accounts/[id]`, a conversation-history page. It does not contain CRM details, ownership, custom fields, pipeline or opportunity controls.
- Conversation cards and successful transcript imports keep review inside the conversation workspace; they do not route users into the legacy meeting details and opportunity fields.
- `/conversations/[id]` is one workspace with Preparation, Notes & capture, Review & follow-up and Relationship history. The old `/workspace` URL redirects to its capture section. Visited sections stay mounted so changing section does not stop capture or discard local text. Completed capture no longer opens a second debrief editor in the capture section.
- Adding a conversation requires a title and conversation type. Company is optional; scheduled time is collapsed. A simple note opens directly in the editor, and rough notes can be saved before starting capture. The core form neither loads nor assigns opportunities. Legacy forms remain available only in their existing legacy routes.
- Preparation works without a company or opportunity. When a sales framework is selected, its unanswered fields provide questions. Confirmed initiative history contributes reviewed evidence from earlier conversations; a different initiative at the same company is not silently combined. Event notes do not automatically receive sales qualification questions.
- Suggested live questions respond to customer-attributed provisional signals and related unanswered preparation questions. The user can inspect the signal and transcript segment reference. Dismissed, superseded, uncertain-speaker and unsupported signals do not drive these suggestions. Suggestions stop when live processing stops. This is bounded rule-based guidance, not generative product answers or a claim of full Backdrop coaching parity.
- Executive Summary prompt version 2 accepts up to 12,000 characters of saved rough notes only for audited owner-private automatic draft jobs. The transcript remains the source of factual claims; rough notes guide emphasis and cannot establish buyer commitments. Ordinary shared jobs never query private notes. Version 1 remains executable for already queued work. The saved notes are loaded at execution; they are not a separately pinned historical note revision.
- Rough notes are saved before explicitly ending a conversation when they differ from the saved workspace. A failed save leaves the text visible and blocks completion until resolved. User-edited enhanced notes and follow-up drafts retain the existing optimistic-locking and explicit-version-application behaviour.

## What must be demonstrated before calling the whole product complete

| Capability                    | Current boundary                                                                                                  | Acceptance still required                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Quiet desktop capture         | Web microphone and supported shared-tab capture require deliberate action; no native application                  | Signed macOS and Windows installers, supported-app call detection, visible armed/recording states, system and microphone audio, sleep/reconnect/device-change tests, consent and stop controls         |
| In-person and phone notes     | Typed notes, authorised imports and supported explicit capture paths                                              | Device-specific validation; do not promise automatic cellular-call interception or universal browser system audio                                                                                      |
| Granola-style enhanced notes  | Private notes can guide transcript-grounded summaries; deterministic provider is used locally                     | Evaluate real output on consented synthetic audio, corrections, speaker attribution, long meetings, note emphasis, retry behaviour and user edits                                                      |
| Backdrop-style sales guidance | Framework preparation, provisional signal detection and bounded next-question suggestions                         | Generative objection handling and product answers from approved business knowledge; useful latency and low-interruption behaviour during real supported calls                                          |
| Meeting feedback and scoring  | Existing framework evidence coverage distinguishes confirmed, partial, unknown, conflicting and stale information | An explained per-call assessment rubric, selectable framework/goals and calibration against transcript evidence; coverage is not a win probability or an independently validated meeting-quality score |
| Business learning             | Existing reviewed website/file knowledge ingestion and gated adapters                                             | Real approved website ingestion, clear correction/reapproval and evidence that business-specific language shapes coaching                                                                              |
| Before-meeting preparation    | Existing briefing preparation and deterministic local outbox                                                      | Activated calendar/research/email paths, timezone-aware delivery, deduplication and cancellation in the target environment                                                                             |
| Long relationships            | Confirmed initiative history separates unrelated work and retains evidence dates/conflicts                        | Owner review of several conversations over months/years, changing stakeholders, dormant relationships and parallel initiatives; no fixed sales-cycle assumption                                        |
| Search and knowledge          | Conversation/account navigation and existing scoped Ask/knowledge paths                                           | Notes-content search, cross-meeting retrieval and sharing must be verified end to end; title search alone is not full Granola parity                                                                   |
| Simple daily use              | One conversation route and no CRM controls in the core account journey                                            | Desktop and mobile visual review, real browser acceptance and owner usability review                                                                                                                   |

Native applications and live-provider activation are separate delivery milestones. A green web build does not establish those capabilities. Do not market the current gated synthetic demo as a complete combination of both competitors.

## Boundaries and rollout

This change does not activate providers, send email, alter billing/Credits, reset customer/demo data, merge to main or deploy production. It introduces no database migration. Source-level tests and synthetic fixtures establish behaviour; live integration claims require target-environment evidence. Roll back by reverting this change; existing records and the legacy routes remain available.

Reference products supplied by the owner: [Backdrop](https://www.getbackdrop.ai/) and [Granola](https://www.granola.ai/). The acceptance contract is based on the owner's requested workflow and the audited implementation, not a promise to reproduce every competitor feature.
