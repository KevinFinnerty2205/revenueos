## 1. About this Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Management Services Australia Pty. Ltd. (we, us or Oryntela) handles personal information in connection with the Oryntela website, sales platform, support channels and related business activities.

Oryntela is a business-to-business sales platform intended primarily for Australian organisations. The organisations that use Oryntela decide which authorised users, business contacts, sales records and connected services form part of their workspace. Business information can still be personal information when it identifies or is reasonably capable of identifying an individual, such as an employee, prospect, customer contact or Deal Room recipient.

This Policy should be read with the Oryntela Terms & Conditions and any collection notice presented for a particular feature. A customer may also have its own privacy obligations and policy. This Policy does not replace those obligations.

## 2. Information we collect and hold

The personal information we collect and hold depends on how Oryntela is used and which capabilities are enabled. It may include the following categories.

### 2.1 Account and organisation information

We may handle names, business email addresses, roles, organisation membership, authentication and session references, access permissions, account status, and records of administrative actions. Authentication providers may handle credentials and security checks directly. Oryntela does not store plaintext passwords.

### 2.2 Sales and relationship information

Customers and authorised users may provide business contact details, company information, roles, relationship notes, opportunities, tasks, pipeline stages, targets, forecasts, methodologies, selling profiles, decisions, risks, action items and other sales-work records. This information can relate to customer personnel, prospects, buyers, partners and other professional contacts.

### 2.3 Customer-supplied content and evidence

Depending on enabled features, customers may deliberately supply meeting or interaction notes, plain-text transcripts, documents, emails, visual material, presentation inputs, business-case assumptions, handover content, feedback and support material. Oryntela does not implicitly listen or record. Any supported recording or capture requires a deliberate user action and appropriate authority or consent.

The Service is not designed for passwords, complete payment-card details, government identifiers, health information, other sensitive information, or unrelated personal files unless a feature expressly supports the category and the customer has appropriate authority.

### 2.4 Connected mailbox, calendar and CRM information

If a customer chooses to connect a supported third-party service when the integration is available, we may process the bounded information needed to provide it. This may include selected CRM accounts, contacts, opportunities and mappings; an approved outgoing email and its delivery status; a strongly correlated reply; or relevant calendar event details.

The intended architecture does not indiscriminately copy an entire mailbox or retain raw third-party provider payloads. Private calendar information is minimised where supported. A customer’s connected provider remains a separate service governed by that provider’s terms and privacy practices.

### 2.5 Prospect and research information

If Prospect or research features are enabled, we may handle professional company and contact information from customer instructions, authorised providers, and public or business sources. Provider availability, permitted use, source and provenance controls apply. The existence of integration code does not mean a production prospect-data provider is active.

### 2.6 AI inputs and outputs

When an AI-assisted feature is used and external AI processing is enabled, we may send selected and bounded inputs needed for that request to an AI service provider. Those inputs can include meeting text, names, employers, roles, business contact details, customer-entered context, selected evidence or reviewed content. We hold validated outputs and limited usage or request metadata needed to operate, secure and monitor the feature.

We do not send all Customer Data to an AI provider. Oryntela configures supported OpenAI request paths with storage disabled for the response object, where documented, but this configuration is not a promise that a provider retains nothing. Provider safety, abuse-monitoring, security and legal retention may still apply under the provider’s terms. Oryntela does not use identifiable Customer Data to train a general-purpose AI model unless a separate, explicit policy and lawful agreement permits it.

### 2.7 Deal Rooms and reviewed materials

Customers may select information to publish through a Deal Room, including approved summaries and pinned presentation material. We process the published snapshot, controlled-link digest, expiry or revocation settings, and limited security or access information needed to operate and protect the Deal Room. A person with the bearer-style link may access the published material without an Oryntela account until access ends.

Closed-Won Handover and other reviewed internal sales records remain in the customer workspace unless the customer separately chooses to publish, send, export or connect them.

### 2.8 Billing, Subscription and Credit information

We may hold plan and Subscription selections, billing contact details, limited payment status, invoice references, amounts, currency, paid-through dates, cancellation or plan-change state, and Oryntela Credit balances and ledger records. Hosted payment-provider pages handle card details. Oryntela does not receive or store full card numbers or card security codes, although the payment provider may do so under its own terms.

Credit records are commercial account and usage records, not a bank account or cash balance.

### 2.9 Usage, device, security and support information

We may collect IP address and request information, timestamps, device or browser information, feature and usage events, error and performance data, audit or history records, security signals, support correspondence, feedback, and records needed to investigate an incident or request. We design logs and audits to minimise customer content and exclude credentials, authorisation headers, raw transcripts, prompts, signed links and full provider payloads.

## 3. How we collect information

We may collect personal information:

- directly from an individual who creates an account, uses Oryntela, contacts us or participates in support;
- from a customer or another authorised user who enters, imports or shares business information;
- from a customer-authorised mailbox, calendar, CRM or other connected service when that integration is enabled;
- from a payment, identity, hosting, AI, support or other service provider involved in delivering an enabled capability;
- from public or licensed business sources when an approved Prospect or research capability is available; and
- automatically through necessary application, security, audit and technical records when a person uses the website or Service.

Customers are responsible for having appropriate authority and giving required notices before they provide another person’s information or connect a system to Oryntela. Importing professional contact information does not by itself establish consent for marketing.

If we cannot collect information that is reasonably necessary for an account or requested capability, we may be unable to provide that account, feature, integration, support or transaction.

## 4. Why we handle information

We collect, hold, use and disclose information where reasonably necessary to:

- create, authenticate, administer and secure accounts and organisation access;
- provide sales, relationship, CRM, pipeline, intelligence, creation, Deal Room, handover and other selected workflows;
- process customer-authorised integrations and reconcile provider outcomes;
- produce requested AI-assisted analysis and reviewed materials when enabled;
- administer trials, Subscriptions, payments, invoices, plan limits and Credits;
- provide support, respond to requests, manage complaints and communicate service information;
- maintain, troubleshoot, measure and improve the reliability, accessibility, security and usefulness of Oryntela;
- detect, prevent and investigate fraud, abuse, security incidents and breaches of our Terms;
- perform tenant isolation, backup, recovery, export, deletion and business-continuity activities;
- establish, exercise or defend legal claims and meet accounting, tax, regulatory and other legal obligations; and
- generate genuinely aggregated or de-identified operational insights for security, service analytics, capacity planning and improvement.

We do not sell personal information. We do not grant ourselves a broad right to commercialise identifiable Customer Data. We do not use a customer’s name, logo, testimonial or case study in marketing without separate permission.

## 5. Customers’ outreach and Oryntela communications

Customers control their own Engage and sales outreach. They are responsible for recipient selection, authority or consent, message content, sender identification, suppression and unsubscribe requirements, and compliance with applicable electronic-marketing laws. Oryntela may process the information needed to carry out a reviewed or otherwise authorised workflow, but does not guarantee that a customer’s communication is legally compliant.

We may send customers and authorised users necessary account, security, billing, support and service communications. We may also send Oryntela business or product communications where permitted. Recipients can use the unsubscribe mechanism in a marketing message or contact us, although we may still send non-marketing communications needed for an active account or transaction.

## 6. When we disclose information

We may disclose personal information only as reasonably needed for the purposes in this Policy, including to:

- the customer that controls the relevant workspace and its authorised users;
- a recipient the customer deliberately chooses, such as a Deal Room recipient or recipient of approved outreach;
- hosting, storage, backup, identity, email, payment, support, security, monitoring and professional service providers;
- AI service providers for selected requests when an external AI feature is enabled;
- customer-authorised Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, HubSpot, Salesforce or other integration providers when the relevant integration is enabled;
- an approved professional-data or research provider when Prospect features are available and used;
- regulators, courts, law-enforcement bodies and other persons where required or authorised by law; and
- a genuine prospective purchaser, investor or successor in a corporate transaction, subject to appropriate confidentiality and lawful handling.

Our proposed launch provider set may include DigitalOcean for Sydney-region core hosting, Amazon Web Services for an independent encrypted backup, Clerk for identity and organisation access, Zoho for business and support email, a payment provider such as Stripe, and an AI provider such as OpenAI. These providers are not all active during this owner-review draft. Before this Policy is approved, we will reconcile the list against the providers actually enabled for launch. Listing a conditional or proposed provider does not mean it receives information when its capability is disabled.

We require providers to handle information for the contracted service and apply appropriate safeguards. Their own infrastructure, subcontractors and legal obligations may also affect processing.

## 7. Overseas processing and disclosure

Core application infrastructure may be located in Sydney, but Oryntela does not claim that information is processed only in Australia. Depending on the enabled provider, customer connection, support path and the provider’s current infrastructure or subprocessors, information may be processed or disclosed outside Australia.

Countries can vary by provider and feature. Before final approval, we will identify likely overseas countries where practicable based on the providers actually enabled for launch. Overseas recipients may be subject to different laws. We take reasonable steps appropriate to the relationship and applicable requirements when selecting and contracting providers, but cannot control the laws applying to an overseas recipient.

## 8. Security

We use technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the information and Service. They include server-side access checks, organisation and tenant separation, restricted support and administrative operations, encrypted provider credentials, HTTPS for production connections, private storage, controlled and revocable Deal Room links, bounded provider requests, review and approval controls, audit or history records, data minimisation, safe error handling, and backup and recovery controls.

No system connected to the internet is completely secure. We do not claim perfect security or guarantee that an incident will never occur. Customers also contribute to security by controlling authorised users, protecting accounts, reviewing connected services and promptly reporting suspected compromise.

We assess and respond to suspected data breaches under our incident processes and applicable obligations. Where notification is required, we will notify affected people and regulators in the manner required by law rather than promising an arbitrary fixed timeline.

## 9. Retention, export and deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to provide and secure the Service, administer the customer relationship, support export or recovery, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and meet accounting, tax, regulatory and legal obligations. Different categories may therefore have different retention periods.

Organisation data may be subject to a customer or deployment retention setting. Operational settings, proposed 30, 90 or 180-day choices, and a 90-day engineering default are not a promise that every category is retained for the same period. The production policy must match the configuration actually approved for launch.

Supported organisation exports are tenant-scoped and may be provided through authenticated, time-limited access. An export covers supported Oryntela records and formats; it does not guarantee export in every conceivable format or direct compatibility with every third-party platform.

When an authorised deletion is completed, we delete or de-identify information from active Oryntela systems subject to technical feasibility, legitimate records and applicable obligations. Deletion from backups occurs through the applicable backup rotation rather than instantaneously. Restricted billing, security, audit, fraud, dispute or legal records may be retained for as long as reasonably required. Information in a customer-controlled third-party service is governed by that service and is not necessarily deleted when Oryntela is disconnected or deleted.

For information controlled by an Oryntela customer, we may refer the request to that customer or coordinate with it, while still meeting any obligations that apply directly to us.
