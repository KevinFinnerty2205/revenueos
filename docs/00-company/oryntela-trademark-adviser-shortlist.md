## 4. Proposed first engagement

Request a written, professional Australian trade mark clearance and risk advice
for the `ORYNTELA` word mark as proposed for B2B SaaS and AI-assisted sales
software. The fixed scope should include:

1. Australian register searching and advice on substantial identity, deceptive
   similarity and registrability;
2. specific verification and legal significance of `ORYNTECH` EU 019358574 and
   `ORYNTE` Türkiye 2025-080152;
3. Australian unregistered/common-law use, passing off and Australian Consumer
   Law risk proportionate to the proposed use;
4. the correct applicant and ownership position;
5. classes and an appropriately bounded specification, including whether class
   42 is sufficient and whether classes 9 or 35 have a present basis;
6. a word-mark-first strategy and whether further irreversible public brand
   investment should remain paused; and
7. a proportionate Australia-first filing sequence and, only if commercially
   relevant, later Madrid or direct international options.

opposition, rebrand or other work is authorised as part of the quote request.

## 5. Key issues the adviser must answer

1. Is `ORYNTELA` available to use and register in Australia for the actual
   proposed product, and on what limitations or conditions?
2. What is the present status, owner, scope and practical Australian significance
   of `ORYNTECH` EU 019358574 and `ORYNTE` Türkiye 2025-080152?
3. What deceptively similar, phonetic, conceptual, historical and unregistered
   uses require further investigation?
4. Could the proposed use create passing-off or Australian Consumer Law risk even
   if no blocking Australian register mark is found?
5. Is the proposed applicant, Management Services Australia Pty. Ltd., the
   correct owner, and what adoption, creation, first-use or authorised-use records
   should be preserved?
6. Which classes and exact goods/services are supportable now? Is class 42
   sufficient, and is there a real current basis for classes 9 or 35?
7. Should the plain word mark be prioritised before any device/logo mark?
8. Should further irreversible public-facing brand investment remain paused until
   clearance is complete?
9. Is Australia-first appropriate, and what future priority or Madrid deadlines
   would arise only if an Australian application is later authorised?
10. What further work, cost and timing would be required before an application or
    public launch decision?

Every answer **requires qualified trade mark advice**.
