## Decision

**Recommended first contact: Sprintlaw, attention Alex Solo.**

Sprintlaw is the best first quote target for this bounded pre-launch review. Alex
Solo's current practising status and principal role were independently verified, his
published background is directly relevant to technology contracts, and Sprintlaw has
specific SaaS, privacy, data and AI work rather than a general small-business-only
offering. Its fixed-fee online process, relevant public Privacy Policy price and
published five-working-day norm provide better cost and timing signals than the other
established candidate. The quote must still confirm who will do the work, the combined
price, GST-inclusive total and that the existing drafts will be reviewed rather than
replaced with generic documents.

Two alternatives are retained. Clever Legal Counsel has the clearest public
line-item pricing and AI-specific scope, but its current legal entity is very new,
its commercial-practice public materials are recent and its figures are expressly
indicative. Prosper Law has a credible established commercial/privacy principal and
fixed-fee model, but publishes neither a SaaS-specific review price nor a delivery
period for this combined scope.

## Scope and method

This is a shortlist of three, not an endorsement or a procurement decision. It was
prepared against the existing:

- [WO-054 Privacy and Terms professional-review package](wo-054-privacy-terms-professional-review-package.md);
  and
- [WO-054 draft Privacy Notice and Terms inputs](wo-054-draft-privacy-and-terms.md).

The comparison prioritises Australian practising status, B2B SaaS and privacy work,
AI/data relevance, a willingness to review an existing factual package, fixed or
capped pricing, turnaround and likely minimum cost. Practitioner status was checked
on the applicable official state register. Firm websites support experience, service,
fee and contact claims; those marketing claims were not treated as independent proof
of practising status.

No price below is presented as the price for Oryntela. A fee is shown only where the
firm publishes it for a directly relevant service. Website/e-commerce terms are not
treated as equivalent to Oryntela's B2B SaaS Terms, and separate line items are not
silently converted into a combined quote.
