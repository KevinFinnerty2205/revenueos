## Terms acceptance production gap

Repository inspection found product acknowledgements for specific data and capture workflows, but no durable general acceptance record for the Oryntela Terms and no distinct Privacy Policy acknowledgement in account, trial or paid-subscription onboarding. The legal documents and public links do not by themselves prove agreement. This remains a WO-054 production activation gap; it is not implemented by this drafting change because it requires a coordinated tenant-owned API, schema and onboarding/checkout change.

The minimum production change is:

- show the effective Terms, Privacy Policy, pricing, renewal and cancellation information before trial activation and paid checkout;
- require the organisation representative to affirm authority and accept the Terms before trial or paid activation;
- store an append-only tenant-scoped record containing Terms version, effective date, acceptance timestamp, accepting user, organisation and acceptance surface or Order reference;
- record Privacy Policy version and acknowledgement timestamp separately from contractual acceptance where acknowledgement is required;
- block activation when the required current record is absent and preserve prior records after document updates; and
- test cross-organisation denial, idempotency, version changes, audit minimisation, keyboard use and safe failure.

This does not require a legal-document management system. Live checkout, production activation and real customer data remain blocked until this evidence path and the other WO-054 gates are approved and verified.
