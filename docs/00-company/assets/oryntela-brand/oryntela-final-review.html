09 · Usage rules

Restraint is part of the identity.

A small system is easier to protect and easier to use consistently.

Clear space

Let x equal the meridian stroke width. Keep at least 1.5x clear on every side of every logo variant.

Minimum size

Horizontal: 144px wide.
Wordmark: 96px wide.
Symbol: 16px square.

Never
  • stretch, rotate or restyle;
  • add glow, gradient or shadow;
  • retype the wordmark;
  • make copper dominant; or
  • use low-contrast backgrounds.
