# Phone-call debrief

RevenueOS does not intercept, monitor or record a phone call. After the call is marked
completed, the salesperson can start AI Debrief or Voice Journal from the Interaction.

For calls of three minutes or less the opener is **What changed?** with at most one
follow-up. Normal calls use at most four questions, high-value linked opportunity
calls of at least 15 minutes may use five, and Voice Journal remains capped at two.
The questions prioritise outcome, next step and commitments, then material opportunity
gaps. The user may finish early.
Every extracted statement is labelled “Reported by you” and must be edited, accepted
or rejected before it updates Interaction Intelligence.

Recommended demonstration: complete a synthetic phone-call Interaction, answer with
the outcome and a commitment, edit one candidate, reject another, accept the rest and
verify the source-aware Opportunity Workspace section after refresh.

No-answer, voicemail and cancelled calls can retain a one-answer seller note but do
not create customer Interaction Intelligence.
