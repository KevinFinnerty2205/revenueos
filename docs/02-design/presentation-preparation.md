# Presentation preparation

## Current experience

Presentation preparation emphasises audience/stakeholder context, the interaction
objective, questions and evidence likely to be useful, unresolved objections and an
observable validation step. The responsive brief labels its specialised guidance
clearly.

No deck upload or document processing is implemented by WO-012. If seller-prepared
material is introduced later, it remains preparation context and cannot support a
customer buying signal, objection, commitment or decision by itself. Current
composition uses only linked metadata and existing validated structured
intelligence.

## Safe outcome

A useful presentation clarifies audience questions, requested evidence and the next
validation step. It does not treat attendance, seller claims, eye contact or generic
politeness as evidence of customer intent.
