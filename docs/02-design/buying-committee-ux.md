# Buying Committee UX

Buying roles are presented as cards headed **Hypothesis — Needs validation**, paired with the trust label, rationale and public sources. Wording uses “may”, “possible” and “candidate”; it never says a person is the decision-maker without customer evidence.

The seller can mark a hypothesis relevant or not relevant. This is an assessment of research usefulness, not confirmation of stakeholder authority. The page explicitly states that reviews do not change Stakeholder Intelligence.

The company-level view can show bounded coverage gaps, for example a missing Security function. Gaps are prompts for further research and are never scored or treated as deal risk. Unusual job titles may be normalised into the fixed role vocabulary, but the underlying public title and rationale remain visible.

The simplicity rule is one primary next action per state: find people, research a selected person, review a hypothesis, or explicitly add the person to Sales. No org chart, network graph, lead score or automated stakeholder creation is included.
