# Business Case UX

The representative flow uses progressive sections rather than a spreadsheet:

1. customer and approved model;
2. labelled input cards with unit, bounds, origin and lock state;
3. neutral result cards;
4. “Why this number?” disclosures with formula and actual dependent inputs;
5. provenance table, scenarios and optional sensitivity;
6. exact-version approval and presentation handoff.

Negative currency and ROI remain visible without red/green scoring. Payback with non-positive benefit reads **Not achieved under these assumptions**. Results are described as modelled estimates, never guaranteed outcomes.

Loading, first-use, empty, error, stale-source and approval states use semantic headings, landmarks, labels and status/alert regions. Formula disclosures are keyboard-native `<details>` controls. Input provenance becomes labelled stacked cards on narrow screens and a comparison table on larger screens.
