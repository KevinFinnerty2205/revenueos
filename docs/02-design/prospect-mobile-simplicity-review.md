# Prospect mobile UX and simplicity review

**Status:** Passed for WO-026

The mobile journey remains: Search → choose company → brief → Add to Sales. The
brief prioritises company identity, why this may matter, recent developments and
the promotion action. More research, sources and history remain lower on the page.
Cards wrap vertically; there are no wide tables or horizontal source-management
controls.

The implementation review found:

- Find explains its purpose and company search is the obvious first action.
- Ambiguity requires a visible choice; no candidate is auto-selected.
- Trust is textual and each material fact exposes its source.
- Add to Sales explains the exact-domain duplicate outcome before confirmation.
- Public research is explicitly separate from customer Evidence.
- Partial, failed and non-entitled states provide restrained next steps.
- Refresh is available without competing with promotion.
- Provider and worker terminology is absent from the customer experience.
- No second mobile primary-nav item was added; the existing mobile shell stays
  simple.
- The page answers “is this company worth pursuing?” with facts and hypotheses,
  not a score.

Playwright covers the compact viewport and the same semantic controls as desktop.
The screenshot review is recorded in the WO-026 sprint record.
