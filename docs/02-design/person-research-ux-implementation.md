# Person Research UX implementation

The company research page adds one secondary section, **People worth understanding**, without changing primary navigation. The seller explicitly starts discovery; page loads never trigger provider fetches. Results show name, public role, company, relevant function, a short “why” and research status. No photo, score or popularity ranking appears.

The person page follows the established Account Research visual language. Its hierarchy is: identity and status; why this person may matter; professional profile; possible buying-committee role; professional activity; known business contact details; changes; sources; history. Trust labels and source links sit next to the claim they qualify.

Loading, pending, partial, failed, departed and unknown-contact states use plain language. “Role may have changed” is prominent when employment support disappears. Contact details are plain text, not action links, and always show permission not assessed.

Promotion opens a confirmation dialog that states the exact limited mutation. Possible duplicate Contacts are reviewed before create/attach. The promoted Contact exposes a separately labelled **Public professional research** link; the brief does not blend into Contact fields or customer truth.
