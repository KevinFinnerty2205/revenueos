# Forecast experience

**Entry:** Insights → Forecast. Opportunity Workspace contains a contextual link back
to this review surface.

The screen orders information by decision value: calendar month/quarter and currency
filters; Actual/Target/Seller Commit/RevenueOS baseline; inclusive seller range;
separate historical baseline; input-quality disclosure; deal review; calibration.
This hierarchy makes the different authorities visible before detail.

The historical baseline is never labelled the forecast range. Deal cards reveal the
exact sample and calculation in a native disclosure. Loading, empty and safe retry
states preserve the filter context. Admins may choose organisation or owner scope;
members see their own aggregate. No probability, weighted pipeline, rank or score is
shown.
