# Phone-call preparation

## Current experience

`phone_call` is a first-class AI Companion preparation type. The mobile layout puts
the concise objective and suggested questions first, followed by stakeholder role,
prior open commitments, risks, success criteria and a recommended close. Suggested
questions are capped at five.

The brief is intended for a quick scan before dialling. It does not provide a dial
button, telephony, cellular interception, VoIP, recording or live coaching, and it
does not assume that call recording is available.

## Content rules

- Prefer one concrete next-step objective.
- Surface only validated open commitments; do not infer completion or owner.
- Use validated stakeholder intelligence or linked participant metadata only.
- Ask short, customer-appropriate questions about unresolved areas.
- Close with an observable next step, owner and timing—not a probability or deal score.
