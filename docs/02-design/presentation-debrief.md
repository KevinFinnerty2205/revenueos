# Presentation debrief

Presentation debrief captures customer reaction, not the seller’s prepared material.
After the Interaction is completed, AI Debrief starts with “How did it go?” and may ask
which areas generated discussion, what objections or questions emerged, and what next
step was agreed. Existing Pre-Interaction Brief questions are considered without
turning the flow into a generic checklist.

Voice Journal is available for a short natural summary with typed fallback. It does
not record the presentation or the customer. All candidates remain “Reported by you”
until the salesperson reviews every item; no silence or remembered reaction becomes a
customer-confirmed claim.
