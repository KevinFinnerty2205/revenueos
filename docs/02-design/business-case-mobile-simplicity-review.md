# Business Case mobile and simplicity review

## Findings

- The seller never sees an empty grid; every field has a business label, unit, approved bounds and origin.
- Results precede the longer provenance table on a calculated case, while formulas remain one disclosure away.
- Cards collapse to one column; scenario cards and approval remain usable at phone width.
- Wide provenance content uses an explicit horizontal scroll region rather than clipping.
- Model administration is available on small screens but intentionally desktop-first; seller review/approval is the mobile priority.
- No colour alone communicates positive/negative or approval state.

The workflow passes the simplicity gate: missing values are obvious and block calculation; defaults are visible; provenance and formulas are inspectable; negative outputs are honest; scenario changes are explicit; and the approved presentation handoff is one action. It is materially smaller than an ad hoc spreadsheet while retaining every calculation dependency.
