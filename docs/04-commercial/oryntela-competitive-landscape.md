# Oryntela competitive landscape

- **Status:** Internal strategic comparison, not public claims
- **Research date:** 4 September 2026
- **Method:** Official vendor product/pricing/support material; no account or trial

## Is monday CRM a competitor?

**YES — an overlapping competitor**, especially for small and mid-market teams
choosing a CRM plus sales workflow and AI. monday CRM overlaps Oryntela in lead and
deal records, pipelines, email/activity history, sequences, automation, dashboards,
forecasting, targets, mobile access, AI assistance, meeting notes and emerging agents.

monday's current strength is a mature, flexible CRM/work platform with broad
configuration, automation and integrations. Oryntela should not try to become a more
configurable monday. Its intended distinction is an opinionated Evidence -> Sales
Brain -> Methodology -> Action -> Pipeline/Forecast loop that asks sellers to
configure less, preserves source authority and deliberately rejects employee/buyer
surveillance patterns.

The implication is not a parity sprint. Validate whether Oryntela's connected,
evidence-backed workflow removes more sales administration for the target team than
a configurable CRM. Keep integration/coexistence possible and make simplicity a
structural product choice.

## monday CRM pricing snapshot

The [official pricing page](https://monday.com/crm/pricing), observed on 4 September
2026, displayed Basic $18 monthly / $12 on the annual toggle, Standard $25 / $17 and
Pro $41 / $28 per seat per month; Ultimate was custom. The extracted page did not
identify the currency, so these are **displayed-dollar figures, not verified AUD
prices**. The page also showed inconsistent saving language (33% near the toggle and
18% in its FAQ). Confirm currency, tax, exact discount and Australian checkout before
any external comparison.

| monday CRM plan | Five-seat annual at annual-toggle figure | Five-seat annual if monthly-billed for 12 months |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------: | -----------------------------------------------: |
| Basic           |                    720 displayed dollars |                          1,080 displayed dollars |
| Standard        |                  1,020 displayed dollars |                          1,500 displayed dollars |
| Pro             |                  1,680 displayed dollars |                          2,460 displayed dollars |

Official support says pricing uses seat buckets starting at three and then multiples
of five, and annual billing is prepaid. Oryntela Core is hypothesised at **AUD
$2,000/year for up to five users**. These figures are not directly comparable without
confirmed currency, required monday tier, AI/Notetaker entitlement, add-ons and tax;
nor do they imply feature equivalence.

Official current product/support material says Pro contains mass email/sequences,
while forecasting is available on Pro/Ultimate. monday also documents email sync,
custom dashboards, automations, iOS/Android, AI fields/assistants, a Lead Agent and
Sales Agent, with some AI actions consuming credits. The pricing and support pages do
not present every AI/Notetaker entitlement consistently, so verify at purchase rather
than publishing a definitive matrix.

## Focused category map

This is a positioning map, not a full current-feature audit or objective ranking.

| Product/category         | Primary strength relative to Oryntela today        | Overlap                                   | Oryntela intended distinction                                    |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| monday CRM               | Configurable CRM, workflow, automation and breadth | CRM, pipeline, email, reporting, AI       | Opinionated sales operating loop with less configuration         |
| Salesforce               | Enterprise CRM ecosystem and depth                 | Records, pipeline, forecast, integrations | Complement it; do not reproduce its breadth                      |
| HubSpot                  | Integrated SMB CRM/marketing/sales platform        | CRM, email, automation, reporting         | Evidence-first Sales Brain and deliberate authority              |
| Attio, Pipedrive, Close  | Modern/simpler CRM workflows                       | CRM, pipeline, communications             | Interaction-to-intelligence continuity                           |
| Gong                     | Mature conversation/revenue intelligence           | Calls, deal risk, coaching                | Multiple deliberate evidence paths and workflow continuity       |
| Clari                    | Forecast/revenue operations depth                  | Forecast and manager intelligence         | Seller workflow plus explainable Evidence continuity             |
| Apollo, ZoomInfo, 6sense | Live data, enrichment, intent/market coverage      | Prospect and decision makers              | Sourced research flowing into one reviewed sales workflow        |
| Clay                     | Flexible enrichment and data workflows             | Prospect research/orchestration           | Bounded opinionated defaults, not a data workflow builder        |
| Outreach, Salesloft      | Production engagement execution at scale           | Outreach, sequences, calls                | Relationship context, restrained communication and human control |

## What to learn and what not to copy

Learn from fast onboarding, coherent communication history, useful mobile access,
clear plan boundaries and visible automation state. Do not copy generic boards,
workflow-canvas complexity, activity surveillance, opaque probabilities, broad
autonomy or a feature-count roadmap. Public claims must be supported by observed
Oryntela outcomes, not competitor marketing language.
