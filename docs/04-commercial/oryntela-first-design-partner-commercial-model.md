# Oryntela first design-partner commercial model

- **Recommendation:** **FREE DESIGN PARTNER**
- **Term hypothesis:** 6–8 weeks
- **Cohort:** One small company; roughly 3–5 active users
- **Status:** Owner/legal approval and signed agreement required; no billing needed

The first partner exists to validate workflow value, trust, simplicity and pricing—not
to extract early revenue or establish final pricing. Oryntela is repository-ready for
a supervised Native CRM partner only after the controlling target/legal/partner gates
pass. It is not ready for unsupervised or commercial beta.

## Offer boundary

Subject to approved legal material, offer no Oryntela licence fee for a fixed period,
supervised onboarding, agreed support hours and weekly feedback. Start with a small
reviewed data subset only after real-data approval. Use external AI only under the
approved feature/provider profile; otherwise present the honest no-external-AI path.

The partner commits to an accountable sponsor/admin, 3–5 active users, onboarding,
weekly candid feedback, review of AI output, minimum-authorised data and immediate
reporting of confusing or contradictory behaviour.

Do not promise custom integrations, migration at scale, 24/7 support, SLA/HA,
autonomous action, a roadmap item, final price, automatic renewal or exclusivity. A
testimonial, logo, reference or case study requires separate written approval after
value is observed.

## What to learn

- whether Oryntela becomes part of the normal sales day;
- preparation/admin time removed and unresolved follow-through;
- trust in Evidence, Sales Brain, Actions, Forecast and Create;
- whether Native CRM/Pipeline is sufficient for the first cohort;
- which context or integration gap repeatedly forces users elsewhere;
- willingness-to-pay range and company-vs-seat preference; and
- support/onboarding burden and likely sustainable cost to serve.

Use interviews, task completion, explicit feedback and content-minimised operational
events. Do not use covert session recording, activity surveillance or rep scoring.

At the end, choose continue, narrow, fix trust/usability, qualify one provider or stop.
Any paid continuation requires a new approved offer and legal review.
