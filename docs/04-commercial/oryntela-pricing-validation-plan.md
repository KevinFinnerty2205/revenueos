# Oryntela pricing validation plan

- **Status:** Approved research approach; no customer outreach or pricing publication authorised
- **Consolidated:** 4 September 2026

## Questions to answer

1. Is company-level pricing easier and fairer than per-seat pricing for the initial ICP?
2. At what price does Core appear suspiciously cheap, good value, expensive but
   plausible, or too expensive to consider?
3. Does AUD $200/month or AUD $2,000/year match the value observed after normal use?
4. Is Growth a coherent upgrade and what outcome belongs in it?
5. Does Complete replace enough work/tools to support AUD $500/month?
6. How many users should be included before a higher band is appropriate?
7. Which external operations create enough value to justify Credits?

## Method

Do not ask only “Would you pay AUD $200?”. First establish the current workflow,
pain, time, tool spend, consequence and observed Oryntela value. Then use four
non-leading price-sensitivity questions: too cheap to trust, good value, getting
expensive, and too expensive to consider. Ask the buyer to compare company bands and
per-seat pricing, monthly and annual commitment, and Core/add-on versus bundle choice.

Record role, team size, current stack, purchase authority, observed usage period,
answer, reasoning and confidence. Do not store unnecessary customer content.

## Evidence sequence

| Stage                    | Evidence                                         | Decision enabled             |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| Design-partner discovery | Current cost/pain and buying process             | Fit and interview language   |
| 6–8-week use             | Time saved, repeated use, trust, support burden  | Core value and cost to serve |
| End-of-term interview    | Price ranges, packaging choice and objections    | Private paid-beta offer      |
| Small paid cohort        | Conversion, retention, support and provider cost | Public pricing candidate     |
| Self-service readiness   | Trial conversion, abuse and unit economics       | Public-trial decision        |

## Decision gates

Do not publish a price until target customer, buyer, included outcome, user band,
provider exposure, support burden, GST presentation, cancellation/refund treatment
and gross-margin risk are understood and owner-approved. Competitor price is context,
not Oryntela's value metric.
